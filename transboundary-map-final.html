Data Sources, Accounting Methodologies & Regulatory Frameworks

Primary trade registries, custom tariff codes (HS 3915), and baseline field studies.

1. UN Comtrade Database Trade Registry

Harmonized System Code HS 3915 (Waste, parings, and scrap of plastics: 3915.10 PE, 3915.20 PS, 3915.30 PVC, 3915.90 Other). Quantifies bilateral mass throughput (metric tons) and customs declared freight values.

2. Basel Action Network (BAN) Field GPS Audit

GPS beacon tracking investigations and port shipping manifest cross-examinations. Monitors compliance with the 2019 Basel Plastic Waste Amendments (Entries Y48, A3250, B3011).

3. China's National Sword Policy (2018) Regulatory Shift

Historical baseline: Prior to 2018, China processed ~45% of global plastic waste imports (~7.3M MT/yr). The 0.5% contamination limit abruptly redirected millions of tons to Southeast Asian ports.

4. OECD & Jambeck et al. Framework Deficit Modeling

Models coastal leakage rates by evaluating municipal solid waste (MSW) collection efficiencies, unlined dumpsite densities, and riparian discharge factors into marine ecosystems.

Field Investigation & Ecological Impact Analysis

Following China’s 2018 National Sword policy, waste brokerage networks rerouted high-volume shipments to Selangor and Penang. Unlicensed rural melting plants release dioxins into agricultural waterways, contaminating prawn aquaculture and coastal estuarine mangrove habitats.
