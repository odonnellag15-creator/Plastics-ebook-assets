Global plastic production now exceeds 430 million tonnes annually, overwhelming municipal infrastructure and driving systemic environmental persistence. Ending this crisis demands far more than incremental waste collection—it requires fundamental material redesign and uncompromised circular economics.

The Asia-Pacific region stands at the frontline of this global challenge, bearing the weight of transboundary polymer trade while pioneering critical breakthroughs in materials science. Tracking the lifecycle from fossil extraction to littoral fragmentation, this monograph synthesizes innovations from the Bioplastics Innovation Hub with the pragmatic policy frameworks required to close the loop permanently.
